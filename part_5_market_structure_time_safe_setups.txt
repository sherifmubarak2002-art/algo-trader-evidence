Timing and look-ahead considerations
A swing may occur on one candle but only be confirmed after subsequent candles. The separate time-safe engine summary records 220 swing highs and 220 swing lows confirmed with a two-bar delay.

The confirmation delay is important because a backtest must not use a swing or signal before it would have been knowable. These summaries come from different pipeline outputs and contain different event counts; they must not be combined as though they were produced by one identical run.

Limitations
- Event counts and setup counts are not equivalent to profitable trades.
- These results do not establish a positive expectancy or a durable trading edge.
- The reported summaries do not, by themselves, prove that every feature, signal, entry, and exit is free of look-ahead bias.
- The next validation stage is to reconcile pipeline outputs, audit signal availability and entry timing, and rerun the backtest before making performance claims.
- No live-trading performance is claimed.

Project lesson
A detected pattern is not automatically a valid entry. Reliable strategy research requires consistent event definitions, confirmation timing, executable entry rules, and independently validated outcomes.
