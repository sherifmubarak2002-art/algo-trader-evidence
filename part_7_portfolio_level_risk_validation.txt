=======================================================

Project: Algorithmic Trading Research and Backtesting System
Evidence date: 2026-11-11

1. LONG-ONLY MULTI-STOCK EXPERIMENT
-----------------------------------
Universe: AAPL, AMD, AMZN, AVGO, GOOGL, JPM, META, MSFT, NVDA, TSLA
Trade records: 179
Wins: 63
Losses: 102
Ambiguous same-bar outcomes: 12
Open trades: 2
Resolved trades: 165
Win rate on resolved trades: 38.18%

The experiment disabled bearish entries and evaluated long-only trade
outcomes across ten stocks. Ambiguous trades reached both stop-loss and
take-profit levels within the same daily candle, so their intraday order
could not be established from the available daily data.

3. CONCURRENT POSITIONS
-----------------------
Maximum simultaneous positions: 4

Event convention: exits are processed before new entries on the same
date. The underlying records use daily dates, so intraday event order
cannot be confirmed.

This measures historical trade overlap. It does not establish that a
fully funded portfolio simulation has been completed.

4. RISK AND CAPITAL-MANAGEMENT COMPONENTS
-----------------------------------------
The risk-management implementation includes:
- Position sizing using account equity and a configurable risk fraction.
- Available-capital constraints on position size.
- Capital reservations before purchases.
- Tracking of available cash and open positions.
- Partial position closures.
- Fee-adjusted cost basis and realised P&L.
- Portfolio valuation using available cash and open-position market value.

Default position-sizing risk fraction: 0.5% of realised equity.

Validation checkpoint:
- 171 automated unit tests passed.
- Test command:
  python -m unittest discover -s src -p "test_*.py" -v

These tests validate the covered software components and behaviours.
They do not prove that the complete historical portfolio simulation
integrates all components correctly.

6. CURRENT VALIDATION LIMITATIONS
---------------------------------
- The existing cumulative R-based portfolio analysis is not a
  cash-based, compounded account equity simulation.
- The 179-trade long-only experiment has not yet been validated through
  one integrated chronological simulation of cash, equity, position
  sizing, overlapping positions, and transaction costs.
- The maximum overlap calculation uses a daily event-order convention.
- The transaction-cost results are sensitivity assumptions, not actual
  broker fee measurements.
- No live-trading performance is claimed.

7. NEXT RESEARCH STAGE — PART 8
-------------------------------
Investigate asset-level differences in the long-only experiment:
- Compare outcome distributions across the ten stocks.
- Examine whether performance differences persist after accounting
  for trade frequency and sample size.
- Investigate trade-level R multiples, expectancy, and drawdown by asset.
- Assess whether asset selection or asset-specific filters could
  improve multi-asset strategy robustness.

The objective is to determine whether the strategy should be applied
uniformly across all assets or whether a more selective asset universe
is justified by the evidence.
