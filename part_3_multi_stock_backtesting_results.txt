PART 3 — FROM PATTERN DETECTION TO MULTI-STOCK BACKTESTING

Project: Algorithmic Trading Research & Backtesting System
Evidence type: Historical, rule-based portfolio backtest

OBJECTIVE
Evaluate whether the rule-based trading approach could be tested consistently across multiple stocks, rather than relying on results from a single ticker.

STOCK UNIVERSE
AAPL, AMD, AMZN, AVGO, GOOGL, JPM, META, MSFT, NVDA, TSLA

AGGREGATE RESULTS

Wins: 108

Win rate on resolved trades: 36.24%
Net result on resolved trades: +26R
Profit factor: 1.137

METHODOLOGY NOTES
- Win rate is calculated using the 298 resolved trades only.
- The strategy's recorded outcomes use R-multiples: winning trades are generally +2R and losing trades -1R.
- The 22 ambiguous same-bar outcomes and 2 open trades are reported separately and are not counted as resolved wins or losses.
- These are historical backtest results, not live trading performance.
- Positive aggregate R does not, by itself, establish a durable trading edge or guarantee future performance. Further validation, including transaction-cost and out-of-sample analysis, is necessary.
