The filter retained 42 of 169 trades (24.85%), reducing trade count by approximately 75.15%. Its combined result was 0R versus +2R for the baseline. Results varied by fold; this experiment did not demonstrate an aggregate improvement.

5. INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS
- Long and short results differed materially in this sample.
- Modeled costs nearly erased the baseline's small positive result.
- The tested machine-learning filter reduced trade frequency but did not improve the combined result across the four folds.
- These findings do not establish live profitability or prove that machine learning cannot help. Further validation, cost assumptions, robustness checks, and execution testing remain necessary.

Source data are maintained in the private/local research project. This public evidence file reports selected results only; it does not include strategy code or raw datasets.
