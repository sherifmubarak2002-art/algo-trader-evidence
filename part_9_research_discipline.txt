PART 9 - FROM BACKTESTING TO RESEARCH DISCIPLINE
================================================

Project: Algorithmic Trading Research and Backtesting System
Evidence basis: Phase 4 strategy diagnosis; Phase 5 research plan and
experiment history; Stage 3B verification reports.

This package reports historical portfolio backtests and research controls.
It does not claim that the strategy is profitable or ready for live trading.

1. PHASE 4 - STRATEGY DIAGNOSIS
-------------------------------
The cash-based portfolio covered 10 US equities. Development and Validation
(D+V) results were modestly positive, but both locked out-of-sample (OOS)
timeframes were negative:

                         Daily       Hourly
  D+V return             +0.42%       +4.39%
  OOS return              -1.22%      -12.07%
  OOS win rate             32.35%       30.11%
  OOS profit factor          0.61         0.54

OOS trading performance was already negative before modeled costs: about
-USD 527 daily and -USD 1,216 hourly. Modeled transaction costs amplified the
losses by approximately USD 135 daily and USD 822 hourly; they were not the
primary cause. Favourable GBP/USD translation then masked part of the trading
losses, adding approximately GBP 255 to the daily result and GBP 122 to the
hourly result. FX translation is a reporting effect, not strategy alpha.

These OOS results were diagnostic only. They were not used to tune or select
the strategy.

Limitations: the OOS sample contains 34 closed daily trades and 93 closed
hourly trades, across a 10-stock universe. The diagnosis also records two
daily final-bar signals that could not be filled and two hourly positions
still open at the end of the data; their eventual outcomes are unknown.
Ticker and regime subsets are smaller still. Costs are modeled assumptions,
not measured live fills, and the backtest does not establish future or live
performance.

2. PHASE 5 - PREREGISTERED RESEARCH FRAMEWORK
---------------------------------------------
The Phase 5 plan defines four hypotheses:

  E1  Directional regime filters.
  E2  Trade management / winner persistence.
  E3  ATR-scaled stops.
  E4  Cross-sectional confirmation.

The plan separates Dev (hypothesis development) from Validation (one-time
candidate selection) and keeps OOS protected. Parameters and treatment
definitions are frozen before evaluation; each hypothesis has a maximum of
five Dev configurations. A canonical Dev run ledger records attempts,
including failed attempts, and active treatments require registration before
execution. Validation candidates are evaluated once after the complete
candidate family is frozen. The OOS registry remains locked.

The preregistered primary comparison is a paired moving-block bootstrap of
calendar-aligned per-bar GBP portfolio-return differences, defined as
Delta = baseline minus candidate. Improvement requires Delta < 0. The plan
specifies fixed block lengths, 10,000 resamples with a fixed seed, at least
20 conservative-equivalent blocks, a one-sided null-centred p-value, an
uncentred one-sided confidence bound, and Holm correction for the declared
confirmatory family. Predefined acceptance also requires effect-size and
trade-metric gates; statistical significance alone is insufficient.

This framework is the research plan, not evidence that all four hypotheses
were run or passed. This package reports no E2 results; E2 appears here only
as a preregistered hypothesis. The actual outcomes reported below are the
two recorded E1 Dev comparisons. Neither is a Validation or OOS result.

3. STAGE 3B - VERIFICATION
--------------------------
The recorded Stage 3B verification summary reports 343 passed tests and 108
passed subtests, with zero failures and zero errors. Overall line coverage
in that summary was 64.7%.

A separate adversarial full-suite run reports 354 passed tests and 108
passed subtests, with zero failures and zero errors. Its documented
safeguards verified:

- A five-attempt Dev configuration cap per hypothesis, including failed
  attempts consuming capacity.
- Rejection of duplicate run IDs and alternate Dev ledger paths.
- Registration before treatment execution; failures consume an attempt.
- Concurrent registration cannot exceed the cap.
- Frozen treatment configurations and disabled-by-default treatments.
- Integrity of protected files, including historical results, market data,
  the Dev ledger and the OOS registry.

The concurrency test used threads against one isolated ledger. It did not
test multiple OS processes or establish cross-host or NFS locking guarantees.
These verification tests establish software-control behaviour, not strategy
profitability or trading performance.

----------------------------------
Both recorded comparisons use Dev data only. The paired mean is Delta =
baseline minus candidate in per-bar fractional GBP portfolio-return units.
The preregistered improvement direction is a negative mean, with the
one-sided p-value and confidence-bound criteria also required.

    Mean paired Delta:                +0.000016960492804308564
    Null-centred one-sided p-value:     0.7534246575342466
    One-sided 95% upper confidence bound:
                                      +0.00005938278084169883
    Conservative blocks:              43 (minimum 20)
    Decision:                         REJECTED

Both candidates failed the preregistered primary improvement direction and
statistical criteria: each paired mean and upper bound were positive, and
each p-value exceeded 0.05. The conservative block-count gates passed, so
these decisions were not classified as sample-block inconclusive. Holm
adjustment was not applicable to these exploratory Dev comparisons because
no confirmatory family rank was declared. Neither candidate advanced to
Validation. No Validation or OOS results were used to select or tune E1.

These results do not establish that the baseline or either candidate has a
positive edge. They document rejected Dev candidates under the recorded
decision rule.
