PART 8 — CASH-BASED PORTFOLIO SIMULATION AND VALIDATION
=======================================================

Project: Algorithmic Trading Research and Backtesting System
Evidence date: 2026-11-17

1. CASH-BASED MULTI-ASSET PORTFOLIO SIMULATOR
---------------------------------------------
The backtesting system progressed from individual trade-outcome
analysis to a chronological, cash-based portfolio simulator.

Universe: 10 US equities
AAPL, AMD, AMZN, AVGO, GOOGL, JPM, META, MSFT, NVDA, TSLA

Key implementation features:
- Long-only execution and whole-share position sizing.
- 0.5% equity risk per trade.
- Cash and available-capital constraints.
- One open position per ticker.
- Next-bar execution after a signal.
- Gap-aware stop-loss and take-profit execution.
- Conservative stop-first handling of ambiguous same-bar exits.
- Commissions, regulatory fees, spread and slippage.
- Dividends, corporate actions and GBP/USD conversion.
- USD trading ledger with GBP portfolio reporting.
- Timestamped, as-of FX conversion.

The simulator does not use leverage or margin. Short entries are
rejected. These are historical simulations, not live trading results.

4. PERFORMANCE DIAGNOSIS
------------------------
A separate analysis investigated the out-of-sample deterioration
without changing the strategy or rerunning the OOS evaluation.

Findings:
- Pre-cost trading P&L was already negative: approximately
  -USD 527 daily and -USD 1,216 hourly.
- Modeled transaction costs increased the losses but were not
  their primary cause.
- Estimated friction was approximately USD 135 daily and
  USD 822 hourly.
- Hourly slippage was the largest modeled friction component.
- Favorable FX translation offset approximately GBP 255 of
  daily losses and GBP 122 of hourly losses.
- FX translation is not strategy alpha.

Ticker-level observations were based on small samples and should
not be treated as robust asset-performance rankings.
